# Investigative Documentary Series: SUNO V6 Score Toolkit

Twelve instrumental style prompts for a premium investigative / true-crime / manhunt documentary series, written for SUNO V6. Every prompt is a single continuous style prompt of at most 1000 characters including spaces, contains no lyrics, no artist or composer references, and is designed to sit beneath narration and interviews.

Shared palette across the series: felt and prepared piano, low chamber strings, bass clarinet, sub-bass pedals, clock-like ticks, tape and room-tone textures, restrained analog pulses. Each cue uses a different subset, tempo, meter, tonal centre and dramatic perspective so no two cues share a fingerprint.

## Usage

Paste the **Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt** text into the SUNO style field with the track set to instrumental. The `Characters` line is the exact count of that prompt text. The `Profile` line is for the editor and music supervisor, not for SUNO.

## Cues

### 1. Cold Open

**Function:** Establish quiet wrongness over the first images, before the viewer is given a single fact.

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text
Instrumental documentary score, cold open cue. Slow and unmeasured, roughly 60 BPM felt as a breath, no clear downbeat. Centre on D: a single felt piano note repeats softly at irregular intervals while a sub-bass pedal on D holds beneath it. Muted chamber strings drift between D minor and D Dorian, the B natural and B flat never settling, voiced low and close so the tonality stays uncertain. Processed room tone and faint tape hiss breathe around the piano. One distant, filtered thud lands far back in the mix, then nothing. A bowed metal harmonic fades in at the top of the register and drifts a few cents flat. No melody, only the repeated note and the chord shifting underneath it. Dynamics stay quiet, one gentle swell in the middle, then a withdrawal into near silence. Dry, intimate close-mic piano against wide, damp reverb tails. Wide open space for narration. No drums, no percussion, no vocals, no risers, no brass, no trailer hits.
```

### 2. Investigation Begins

**Function:** Give the first day of the investigation a methodical, forward-leaning pulse without urgency.

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text
Instrumental investigative documentary score, procedural momentum cue. 92 BPM in 7/8, the missing eighth note keeping the pulse slightly off balance. A muted upright piano plays a dry two-bar ostinato in A minor built from stacked fourths and an added ninth, chords moving Am add9, F major seventh sharp eleven, G sus2, E7 sus4, never resolving. Pizzicato cello doubles the bass notes. Clock-like ticks, a muted rim and a soft wood block, mark the asymmetric groove. A faint analog sub tone underneath, nothing more electronic than that. In the second half sustained violas enter on the suspended intervals, adding harmonic weight but no melody. Arrangement accumulates one layer at a time: piano, then pizzicato, then ticks, then violas, then thins back down to piano alone. Dry, precise mix, midrange left open for narration and interviews, crisp transients, light low end. Focused and methodical rather than tense. No drum kit, no vocals, no brass, no synth leads, no risers.
```

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text

Prepared piano with dampened strings plays isolated single notes and pairs of notes, dry and percussive, separated by long pauses. Granular textures from processed paper, cloth and tape-handling sounds drift across the stereo field as delicate scratches and clicks. No strings and no chords beyond the drone and the intervals the piano notes form against it, mostly minor seconds and major sevenths. Almost no dynamic change, only a slight thickening of the drone midway, then withdrawal. Extremely quiet, intimate and close-mic inside a wide but restrained room. Sits far beneath narration. ### 4. Surveillance

**Function:** Sustain the patience of watching and waiting through camera feeds, stakeouts and night observation.

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text
Instrumental hybrid documentary score, surveillance cue. Steady 100 BPM in 4/4. A low-passed analog sequencer runs continuous sixteenth notes on a single B flat, filter barely open so it reads as a pulse rather than a melody. A sub bass holds B flat with a slow breathing volume swell every two bars. Harmony is static B flat minor; the only movement is an upper voice in tremolo violas, sul tasto, sliding between F and G flat, a semitone that keeps pulling and never resolves. Filtered noise like distant traffic and ventilation hum sits beneath, panned slowly. Small mechanical clicks and camera-shutter ticks land on off-beats. An occasional reverse cymbal swells into silence. Halfway through the sequencer filter opens slightly and a second sequencer line an octave up appears, then withdraws. Restrained, low and patient, the mix kept dark with the midrange clear. No drum kit, no vocals, no risers, no big drops, no melody.

### 5. Unknown Suspect

**Function:** Give the faceless, unidentified perpetrator a psychological presence without writing a villain theme.

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text
Instrumental documentary score, unknown suspect cue. Slow 54 BPM, loose, with generous rubato. Solo bass clarinet plays a three-note fragment, low and breathy, repeated with slight variation and long pauses between statements. A contrabass sustains a low C pedal in natural harmonics. Above it, muted chamber strings hold an E augmented triad, whole-tone colours that never settle into major or minor. Col legno strings tap an irregular pattern, sparse, like someone tapping a table. Reverse-processed piano notes swell backwards into each bass clarinet entry. Faint room tone and a barely audible tape wobble. Development is subtle: the fragment gains a fourth note in the second half, the augmented chord slides up a semitone, then everything drops out except the pedal. Intimate, uneasy, cerebral. Close-mic woodwind, dry strings, deep but controlled low end. Leaves space for interview voices. No drums, no vocals, no horror stingers, no synth pads, no brass, no crescendo.
```

```text
Instrumental hybrid documentary score, manhunt montage cue. 126 BPM in 5/4 grouped 3+2, driving but controlled. Low cellos and contrabass play short staccato cells in G minor with a Phrygian A flat pressing against the tonic. Tight, punchy mix with the voice band kept clear, sub firm but not booming. No braams, no risers, no huge drums, no vocals, no choir, no brass swells, no trailer hits.

### 8. False Lead

**Function:** Build credible momentum behind a suspect or theory, then let it collapse under the viewer.

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text
Instrumental documentary score, false lead cue. 108 BPM, alternating bars of 4/4 and 3/4 so the pulse repeatedly stumbles. Plucked inside-piano strings play a confident D Mixolydian ostinato with the lowered seventh, doubled by pizzicato violas. A bowed vibraphone holds long glassy notes above. A quiet shaker and a soft muted kick keep time. The cue gathers certainty: layers add, register rises, the harmony brightens toward D major. Then the ostinato cuts out mid-phrase, leaving only room tone and a low D flat drone a semitone below, the ground shifting. Strings re-enter sustained and uncertain on a cluster of D flat, E flat and G. The plucked figure tries to return once, slower and incomplete, and stops. Dry, articulate mix with crisp plucks, light low end, midrange open for voices. Built around interruption and silence as much as sound. No drum kit, no vocals, no brass, no synth leads, no risers.
```

### 9. Closing In

**Function:** Tighten the net toward arrest with a slow, controlled acceleration that stays under the narration.

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

```text
Instrumental hybrid documentary score, closing-in cue. 112 BPM in 4/4 with the pulse grouped 3+3+2. A muted sub-bass pulse on every beat, steady throughout. Low cellos in tremolo hold C minor and climb by slow chromatic steps, the bass rising C, D flat, D, E flat over a full minute so tension increases without any louder gesture. Clock-like ticks mark the 3+3+2 grouping. Violas and violins join in tremolo sul ponticello, thin and metallic, layering a diminished chord above the bass. A single distant muted horn holds one note beneath, barely audible, adding weight rather than fanfare. Granular reversed textures swell before each chromatic step. No melody, only ascending harmony and accumulating texture. Sparse start, gradual accumulation, brief thinning at two thirds, then a final held cluster that cuts off instead of resolving. Dark and controlled, clear midrange, firm but restrained low end. No drum kit, no vocals, no braams, no cymbal risers, no trailer hits.
```

**Final SUNO V6 Style Prompt:**

Felt piano repeats a five-note figure in F sharp minor that crosses the barline, accents drifting off the downbeat, circling the tonic without ever landing on it. A soft low-passed analog pulse on eighth notes, its filter opening slowly across the first half. Violas sustain the sixth degree, alternating D natural and D sharp so the mode drifts between Aeolian and Dorian. Tape hiss and gentle room air throughout. A low sub-bass pedal breathes in and out every four bars. In the second half a solo cello adds a short counter-line that asks questions of the harmony rather than answering them. It ends by thinning out: pulse fades, violas drop away, the piano figure repeats twice more and stops on an unresolved note. Warm, clear mix, piano intimate and close, subtle stereo movement in the pulse. Contemplative and steady enough to run under credits. ## Validation Notes

- All 12 prompts pass the mechanical check: at most 1000 characters including spaces, one continuous prompt, single BPM per cue, no composer or artist names, no song references, no lyrics, no repeated sentences inside a cue.
- Only the opening context phrase and the closing exclusion lists are intentionally shared in form between cues; every musical sentence is unique to its cue.
- The `Active Manhunt` and `Closing In` cues are the two high-intensity cues. They were deliberately separated by meter (5/4 staccato cells vs 3+3+2 tremolo ascent), percussion (small dry kit vs no kit) and harmonic mechanism (Phrygian pressure vs chromatic bass climb).
- Percussion, brass and choir are excluded by default and only readmitted where a cue needs them: small dry percussion in the manhunt montage, a single distant muted horn in `Closing In`.
- SUNO does not always honour meter and key instructions literally. If a generation drifts to 4/4 or a neighbouring key, re-run with the same prompt; the instrumentation, tempo and dynamic instructions are the stable anchors.
